# Client Handover and Maintenance

## Handover
- Demonstration of live view, playback and export of a recording
- Mobile app configured on the owner's phone
- Credentials given to the client privately (never stored in this repository)
- Documentation and network diagram delivered

## Maintenance checklist
| Frequency | Task |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Check that all cameras are visible and recording |
| Monthly | Verify disk health and retention period |
| Monthly | Clean camera lenses |
| Quarterly | Check firmware updates (XVR, router) |
| Quarterly | Check cables, connectors and power supplies |
| Yearly | Review passwords and user accounts |

## Quick support
- Camera black: check power, cable/connector, then XVR channel settings.
- Remote view fails: check internet connection, then P2P status on the XVR.
